HOW TO PLAY

Launch and hit an upper N-G-S lane within six seconds for a SKILL SHOT. Time contact near the gold band on each flipper for the cleanest cross-table shot. Complete the TARGET BANK to select a FLIGHT MISSION, then shoot the RAMP to launch it. Follow the dot-matrix instructions before the FUEL lamps run out; correct mission shots extend fuel. Six flight missions earn command flags and promote your rank from CADET toward COMMANDER. A successful pair of route or scoop shots earns one NEON SHIELD replay per turn, giving a productive streak another chance after a drain. The opening ball save now lasts 15 seconds. Each served ball starts with one illuminated side-lane KICKBACK that physically returns a ball from a low outlane; the centre drain remains open. Nudge carefully: the display warns before excessive nudges trigger TILT, which disables flippers and the kickback. Commander unlocks NEON COMMAND at the scoop. To reach NEON OVERLOAD multiball, complete the TARGET BANK, finish or resolve the armed flight mission, then shoot the lit SCOOP to lock a ball. Lock two balls and score a third scoop capture to release three balls. Hit RAMP and ORBIT for jackpots, then SCOOP for the super jackpot. Route shots also charge POWER GRID, and completing the four table objectives awards an extra ball.

SESSION CONTROL
REAL-TIME 3D PREVIEW: Moulded arcade-red plastic, warm-gold guide lips and dark rubber replace the bright metallic guard rails. Orbit and Ramp returns follow outer inlanes around the slings towards the flippers. Centre and Skyline use unobstructed markers. The physics runs in 2D with active lower-wall collisions; raised route surfaces are visual guides, not extra colliders. A 2D fallback is available. No prizes, purchases or real-money play.
