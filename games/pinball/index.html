HOW TO PLAY

Hold and release Launch, then use both flippers to keep the ball alive. Each ball has one 11-second launch safety net for early drains. Complete N-G-S lanes for 2× scoring, hit ten bumpers for Frenzy, clear four targets and connect ramp with orbit for a jackpot. Complete all missions to earn an extra ball.
