HOW TO PLAY

Launch and hit an upper N-G-S lane within six seconds for a SKILL SHOT. Use the ORBIT, RAMP, CENTER CROSSOVER and SKYLINE LOOP for COMBO bonuses. Any three route shots charge POWER GRID; hit the SCOOP within 18 seconds for NEON RUSH JACKPOT. Five spinner crossings start OVERDRIVE for 14 seconds. Complete four missions for an extra ball. Each ball has one 11-second early-drain save.
