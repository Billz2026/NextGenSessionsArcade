LIVE RETAIL CASES
CUSTOMER DEPOSITS & PART-EXCHANGE RULES

A customer enquiry or viewing is not a reservation. A £250 deposit is held separately from business cash until collection; only the remaining balance is collected at handover. Buyer cancellation may allow fair retention for actual customer-specific costs, while dealership fault requires a full refund. Part-exchanges require an agreed allowance and occupy a stock space. No retail sale or revenue is recorded until handover.

LIVE CASH POSITION
HOW TO READ THE FINANCES

Cash balance is actual dealership cash. Cash after repair reserves removes funds earmarked for outstanding vehicle preparation, but not future rent, wages or auction invoices. Stock invested is money already spent on vehicles still held; it cannot be used until a vehicle sells. Gross trade margin reflects completed deals, while operating contribution also deducts recorded staff costs and accrued premises expenses. Customer deposits are held separately. This is a fictional management view, not statutory accounts.
