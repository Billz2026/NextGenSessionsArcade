- **Corroborated** uses a credible UK registered-car/franchise listing or complementary manufacturer/secondary sources where a directly matched UK model-year manufacturer price list is not yet available. Continue source checks before presenting these as maker-certified.
- **Model-year transition check:** Golf GTD 184PS Mk7.5 is distinct from Golf 8 GTD 200PS; Golf 8 GTI 245PS is not the 300PS Clubsport; Golf R Mk7.5 310PS differs from Mk8 320PS. Nissan Juke hybrid is 143PS full hybrid, while Qashqai e-POWER uses its petrol engine as a generator and is not a charge-at-the-plug EV.
- **Model-body and trim check:** Audi A3 Sportback S line is not an S3; Audi RS Q3 Sportback is not an ordinary Q3. Some model variants have hatch/estate, FWD/AWD or manual/DSG splits; require body/drivetrain fields in a later catalogue schema rather than inferring from a common badge.
- **Cash-value gate:** All new prices remain explicitly `unverified`; official new-car list prices in source brochures are *not* 2026 used retail prices, sold prices or wholesale auction hammers.
