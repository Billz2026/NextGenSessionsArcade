SALES OFFICE.

Manage customer enquiries, test drives and deposits. Buyers pay the remaining balance at collection; fair cancellation costs may be retained.

Buyer offers now take the vehicle's likely achieved market value into account. A retail enquiry is not a completed sale. A part-exchange requires an agreed allowance, and the incoming car becomes stock needing workshop preparation. Reservation deposits are part-payments held separately from business cash. Buyer cancellations can forfeit a fair amount for customer-specific costs already incurred; dealer fault requires a full refund. Only the outstanding balance is collected at handover.
